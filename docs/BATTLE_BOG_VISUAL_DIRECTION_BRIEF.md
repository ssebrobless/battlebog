# Battle Bog Visual Direction Brief

Status: user-calibrated direction, pre-prototype

Calibrated: 2026-07-27

Source: exported Battle Bog Visual Reference Quiz completed by the user.

This brief translates the quiz results into visual direction, research scope
and production gates. It does not select the final rendering pipeline.

## North Star

```text
CREATURE LOOK
Pokemon UNITE readability
        +
Temtem: Swarm softness and economy
        -
toy-like containment and plastic surfaces
        |
        v
heroically stylized real wetland animals

COMBAT LOOK
Battlerite precision
        +
SUPERVIVE energy, layering and altitude
        |
        v
clear aim, readable buildup and expressive animal attacks

WORLD LOOK
SUPERVIVE competitive hierarchy
        +
Evercore / Ravenswatch / Wild Woods richness
        |
        v
lush, reactive wetland ecology that remains tactically legible
```

Battle Bog should use highly stylized, dimensional creatures whose real animal
counterparts remain recognizable. Anatomy may be exaggerated aggressively when
that improves attack, movement, height or role readability.

The game should not look like Pokemon UNITE, Temtem, Battlerite or SUPERVIVE
individually. It should combine:

- Pokemon UNITE's ability to keep animal-like creatures readable in team
  combat;
- Temtem's economical creature surfaces and animation readability;
- Battlerite's aim precision, camera stability, anticipation and hit
  confirmation;
- SUPERVIVE's dimensional energy, terrain hierarchy, overlapping height states
  and layered information;
- a richer, more ecological wetland world than any of the clean competitive
  arenas alone provide.

## User-Locked Direction

### Creature Anatomy

- Creatures should be highly heroic and stylized.
- Real species identity must remain recognizable.
- Exaggeration is desirable when it makes attacks, abilities, direction,
  movement or altitude easier to read.
- Models must not all be compressed into one common body envelope.
- Long bodies, wings, necks, tails, swarms and shells need family-specific
  silhouettes.
- Temtem-like rounded containment is too restrictive for real animals.
- Wild Woods creatures are too humanoid.
- V Rising-like hyperrealism is not the target.

### Scale

- Preserve believable size tiers.
- Compress extreme real-world differences enough that small creatures remain
  playable and readable.
- Do not normalize every creature to the same apparent size.
- Collision truth and visible scale remain separate.
- Thin extremities may overhang the truth footprint when their location remains
  honest and readable.

### Movement

- Real-animal locomotion remains a primary authenticity source.
- Pokemon UNITE is useful for clear creature attacks and dives.
- Battle Bog starts and attacks need more buildup and less frantic pacing than
  Pokemon UNITE.
- Battlerite-style technical aim must be integrated without replacing
  species-authentic locomotion.
- Flock is approved for bird banking and continuous flight motion only.
- Rain World is approved for unusual body mechanics and procedural-motion
  principles only.
- Swarms should read as one cohesive mass composed of many individuals.

### Flight And Water Depth

No single cue should carry altitude or submergence.

Use a layered combination of:

- body displacement;
- model scale and pose;
- contact or projected shadow;
- altitude connector;
- ground-projected path or landing point;
- water distortion, wake and silhouette masking;
- transition pose;
- contextual HUD state only when necessary.

SUPERVIVE's overlapping height states are valuable beyond bosses. The same
research should inform underwater creatures rising, diving, striking and
becoming punishable.

### Combat Intensity

- Ordinary combat should sit between Battlerite and SUPERVIVE.
- Battlerite supplies the clarity floor.
- SUPERVIVE supplies an acceptable energy and layering ceiling.
- Spectacle should not be reserved only for bosses.
- Creature identity may justify larger or stranger effects during ordinary
  abilities.
- Basic combat cannot become so restrained that the animal's defining motion
  or attack disappears.
- Hades II is an approved material-impact and painted-quality ceiling.

### Aim And Hit Confirmation

Aim-to-muscle connection is a core quality target.

The player must be able to read:

- current facing and aim direction;
- whether an attack is committed;
- the likely path or occupied area;
- whether contact occurred;
- what body or material made contact;
- whether the target blocked, evaded or was displaced;
- when the attacker enters recovery;
- when a punish window opens.

The Machines Arena is approved as a specialist for crisp projectile language,
cursor-facing poses, camera stability and hit confirmation. Its robotic
materials are not a style source.

### World

- The wetland should feel lush and reactive throughout.
- Environmental richness must not be restricted only to decorative map edges.
- SUPERVIVE's terrain hierarchy should be translated into wetland ecology.
- Pokemon UNITE terrain is too simple and plastic for the desired
  interactability.
- Evercore Heroes is useful for bright dimensional world finish and readable
  units.
- Darker Evercore and Ravenswatch material is useful for nighttime content.
- Wild Woods environments are liked; its humanoid animals are not.
- Water must support convincing submergence, emergence, wakes, distortion and
  depth.
- Death's Door is too structured and architectural to lead the water solution.
- V Rising is too costly, Gothic and materially realistic to lead the world.

### Day And Night

- Night should create a noticeable mood and visibility shift.
- It should not become V Rising-level dramatic physical darkness.
- Navigation, creatures, team truth and fair telegraphs must remain legible.
- Ravenswatch and darker Evercore footage may inform nighttime mood.
- Heroes of the Storm may inform competitive world-state transformation.
- SUPERVIVE may inform information uncertainty without making the world
  physically unreadable.

### HUD

- Provide a simple default HUD and an expanded information mode.
- Ordinary combat should approach Battlerite's quietness.
- Attack, aim, hit and other short-lived information should appear
  contextually.
- SUPERVIVE's information hierarchy is useful after removing inventory and
  revive systems Battle Bog does not use.
- Pokemon UNITE's mobile-sized controls are rejected.
- Omega Strikers' centralized team strip is rejected.
- Dota's configurable depth and visibility information are useful specialist
  references.
- Apex directional warnings and accessibility redundancy are useful specialist
  references.
- Dragon Age switching and allied-AI intent are useful specialist references.

### Boundaries And Rejections

- Pokemon UNITE mobile controls and oversized mobile HUD.
- Pokemon UNITE's simple plastic map treatment.
- Temtem's fully rounded and contained body construction.
- Wild Woods' humanoid creature design.
- V Rising's Gothic direction, dramatic darkness and hyperreal live-3D result.
- Cult of the Lamb's visible illustrated-cutout construction.
- Omega Strikers' centralized sports-team HUD.
- Death's Door's structured, architectural water as a primary water model.
- Eternal Return-level permanent HUD and effect clutter.
- A universal body shape imposed across all creature families.
- A pipeline selected because it is convenient before its visible result is
  compared at gameplay scale.

## Still Provisional

### Rendering Pipeline

The user explicitly remains undecided until direct prototypes.

Current evidence favors testing:

1. Stylized pre-rendered 3D-to-2D atlases.
2. Restrained, non-hyperreal live 3D.
3. A limited 2D-rig comparison whose visible construction must not resemble
   Cult of the Lamb or Don't Starve.
4. Hand-authored key poses as a quality benchmark, not necessarily the roster
   production default.

The prior decision mentioning real 16-pixel sprite sheets must not settle this
comparison by inertia.

### Unresolved Reference Use

- Albion Online's updated wetlands need direct footage review.
- TUNIC may help map traversal and vertical readability, but its diorama
  construction may be incompatible.
- Tribes of Midgard remains an unresolved terrain exaggeration boundary.
- Death's Door may still offer composition lessons outside water behavior.
- Slight V Rising realism may be useful only if isolated from Gothic lighting,
  material cost and hyperreal presentation.
- Don't Starve's modular workflow remains provisional until the prototype
  comparison shows whether equivalent reuse can stay visually invisible.

## Immediate Technical Consequences

1. Ordinary attacks need real startup, active and recovery simulation phases
   before final animation can expose them.
2. The authored renderer must consume existing movement, terrain, flight,
   submergence, aim and attack state rather than replace those systems.
3. Creature production requires family-specific silhouette and motion
   contracts.
4. Air and underwater attacks need the same early-warning and punish-window
   rigor.
5. The environment renderer needs reactive layers that can be reduced or
   suppressed during dense combat.
6. HUD state needs `simple`, `contextual` and `expanded` ownership.
7. Screenshot and video acceptance must occur at the real 1280 x 720 player
   camera, not in model close-ups.

## Acceptance Evidence

- Normal-camera screenshots and continuous video.
- Creature recognition at rest, traversal and crowded combat.
- Tight turns, reversals and aim changes.
- Startup, contact, whiff and recovery alignment.
- Capsule and truth-ring overlays.
- Air and underwater attack reaction tests.
- Six-creature effect and HUD stress tests.
- Day, dusk and night comparisons.
- Grayscale and common color-vision simulations.
- Simple and expanded HUD captures.
- Atlas, build-size, load, memory, draw-call, CPU and GPU measurements.
- Human confirmation that attacks, height, submergence and hit results are
  understood in time to act.

## Originality Rule

Reference mining extracts measurements, relationships and design principles.
It does not authorize tracing, palette sampling, pose copying, animation-frame
replication, texture reuse, UI copying or recognizable style imitation.

Every production brief should combine:

- at least three unrelated game references;
- real-animal evidence;
- Battle Bog's gameplay truth;
- an explicit list of source traits to reject.

## Reference Input Provenance

- Copyrighted `REF` footage and stills become textual measurements,
  relationships and observations.
- Copyrighted frames are not supplied directly to image generation,
  image-to-image, fine-tuning or model-training systems.
- Direct visual model inputs require documented reusable rights, public-domain
  status or project-owned material.
- Every generated or authored production asset records its inputs, authoring
  path, tool/model when applicable and similarity review.
- Public-domain and open-license status is verified at item level before direct
  ingestion; an open institution does not make every hosted item open.
